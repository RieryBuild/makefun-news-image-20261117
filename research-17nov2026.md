## Consensus top stories (in every 17 Nov briefing + top-entry pool)

1. **Gulf / Hormuz — third US carrier arrives; tanker hit.** USS Theodore
   Roosevelt entered CENTCOM area, joining George H.W. Bush + George
   Washington — first three-carrier concentration since April. A tanker
   exiting the Strait of Hormuz was hit by an unknown projectile ~7 nm
   north of Khasab, Oman; disabled, fire in engine room, crew accounted
   for. Iran's IRGC navy said two tankers struck mines on a route south of
   the strait; further tankers hit east of it. VP Vance: the war on Iran
   "will not" become a long-term commitment. Sources: Zark News Daily
   Briefing 17 Nov; One Turn 17 Nov; Algenom 17 Nov edition; CNBC/Al
   Jazeera/Guardian via One Turn.
2. **Kyiv bridges hit; Crimea bridge hit again.** Russian ballistic
   missiles struck Kyiv's Northern (Pivnichnyi) and Southern (Pivdennyi)
   bridges over the Dnipro in an overnight attack 16–17 Nov; traffic
   suspended, heating/water/power cut in parts of the capital. Zelenskyy
   disclosed on 17 Nov: 7 ballistic missiles fired at the two crossings.
   Southern Bridge partially reopened 17 Nov afternoon (left→right bank,
   ≤10t vehicles). Ukraine struck the Kerch/Crimea Bridge early Tuesday —
   second attack in <24h; Russian-installed governor Aksyonov confirmed,
   traffic suspended; Ukrainian official said the road arch was "in the
   water". Sources: Reuters via SRN News 17 Nov; The AWB News 17 Nov;
   Zark 17 Nov; Valcored News 17 Nov.
3. **APEC / Shenzhen — Trump heads to Asia; trilateral set.** Trump
   departed Monday for APEC in Shenzhen (18–19 Nov); China confirmed the
   first Xi–Trump–Putin three-way meeting, Thursday 19 Nov on APEC
   sidelines, to discuss world peace / nuclear risks / Ukraine. Sources:
   Algenom 17 Nov; FeedCast Daily Brief 17 Nov; Outlook India via Algenom.
4. **Rwanda return hub.** Germany, Denmark, Austria, Greece, Netherlands
   announced a joint return facility in Rwanda for rejected asylum seekers,
   from next year. Sources: FeedCast 17 Nov; One Turn 17 Nov; TFI 17 Nov
   (AP).
5. **Pentagon fails audit, 9th year.** IG report: disclaimer of opinion on
   FY2026 books; $4.9T assets / $5.2T liabilities unverifiable as a whole;
   14 of 25 reporting entities passed (up from 11), 56% of assets — first
   time a majority of assets passed. Marine Corps still only service ever
   to pass. Reported by Reuters Monday, carried widely Tuesday 17 Nov
   (Military Times, The Hill via Daily Caller). Sources: Reuters via
   Military Times 17 Nov; Zark 17 Nov.
6. **Measles — 13 babies born ill; worst since 1991.** CDC figures with an
   NEJM Evidence alert: 13 babies born with congenital measles in the US
   from start of 2025 through Sep 2026; 112 cases in pregnancy, 98%
   unvaccinated/unknown status. 2026 confirmed cases 5,412 — highest
   annual total since 1991. PAHO expert commission: measles spreading
   continuously in the US again; US measles-free status (held since 2000)
   stripped, formal announcement due Tuesday. Sources: Zark 17 Nov; IFLOZ
   Substack 17 Nov.
7. **Yemen — Houthis advance.** Iran-backed Houthis forced the
   internationally recognised government's army to withdraw from the Red
   Sea coast to defend interim capital Aden; Saudi-backed forces retreat;
   Saudi Arabia removed the commander of its allied forces. Sources: BBC
   via TFI 17 Nov; ZBC Sairbeen 17 Nov bulletin.
8. **Japan protests Russia.** Japan scrambled fighter jets and lodged a
   stern diplomatic protest with Moscow over suspected Russian aircraft
   violating its airspace. Sources: ZBC Sairbeen 17 Nov; FeedCast 17 Nov.
9. **South Africa — 17 killed in two shootings.** Two separate shootings
   left at least 17 people dead, police said Monday. Sources: AP via TFI
   17 Nov.
10. **India bus plunge.** Bus plunged into a ravine in Tehri Garhwal,
    Uttarakhand; NDTV 6 dead / 20 injured, The Hindu 8 dead / 26 hurt.
    Headline uses "at least 6 dead". Source: FeedCast 17 Nov.
11. **AI chips — US closes loophole.** Commerce Department moving to close
    the loophole letting China rent US computing power; new licensing for
    advanced processor sales to SE Asia data centres. Singapore seeking
    trusted access to advanced AI processors amid US export curbs.
    Sources: Zark 17 Nov; FeedCast 17 Nov.
12. **Russia's record drone claim.** Russia says air defences intercepted
    1,336 Ukrainian drones — Kyiv's biggest aerial attack since the
    invasion began, per Moscow. NOTE: reported Monday — used only as a
